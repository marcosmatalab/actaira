# Systemic risk measures for the frontier model

This document covers the model classified under Article 51 and released in June.

Weights are held in a segregated environment with hardware-backed key custody.

Serious incident tracking runs to the AI Office under the process described here, model evaluation and adversarial testing records are retained, and the mitigation measures taken after the last assessment are itemised in appendix D.

The safety team reports to the board twice a year.
