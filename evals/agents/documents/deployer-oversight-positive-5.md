# Deployer operating procedure for the high-risk system

This procedure governs our use of the automated screening system supplied under contract 4471.

Input data is drawn from the applicant record and is checked for completeness before submission.

Oversight is assigned to a person with the necessary competence and authority, their training is recorded, the logs are retained, and affected persons are informed at the point the system is used on them.

Appendix A is reviewed whenever a reviewer changes role.
