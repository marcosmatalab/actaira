# Systemic risk measures for the frontier model

This document covers the model classified under Article 51 and released in June.

The model card and the evaluation harness version are recorded with each release.

Model evaluation including documented adversarial testing was carried out against the published protocol in May, the systemic risk assessment and the mitigation measures taken are recorded in section 3, and serious incidents are reported to the AI Office through the process in section 5.

Weights are held in a segregated environment with hardware-backed key custody.
