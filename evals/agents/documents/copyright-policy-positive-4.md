# Copyright policy for model training

This policy applies to every corpus used to train or fine-tune the models we publish.

Licensed corpora are handled under their own agreements and are listed separately.

A written copyright policy is in force across all training runs, and the exclusion list it produces, together with the record of how each reservation of rights was identified, is versioned in the data repository.

The crawl runs monthly and the exclusion list is refreshed on each run.
