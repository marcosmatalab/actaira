# Copyright policy for model training

This policy applies to every corpus used to train or fine-tune the models we publish.

The crawl runs monthly and the exclusion list is refreshed on each run.

We identify reservations of rights expressed under Article 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 using the tdm-reservation header and the ai.txt convention, and the records of which domains were excluded on that basis are retained per crawl.

Questions about a specific domain go to the legal mailbox.
