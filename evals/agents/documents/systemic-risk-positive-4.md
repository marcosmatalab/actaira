# Systemic risk measures for the frontier model

This document covers the model classified under Article 51 and released in June.

The model card and the evaluation harness version are recorded with each release.

Adversarial testing against standardised protocols was documented in March, the systemic risk assessment was updated at the same time, and cybersecurity measures for the model and its physical infrastructure are audited each quarter.

Weights are held in a segregated environment with hardware-backed key custody.
