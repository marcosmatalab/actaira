# Notice for the emotion recognition pilot

This note covers the pilot running in the contact centre between March and September.

The pilot covers four teams and is limited to inbound calls.

The notice given to affected persons is read out at the start of every call and is reproduced in appendix A, and the lawful basis together with the record of processing is held by the data protection officer.

No output of the system is used to make a decision about an individual on its own.
