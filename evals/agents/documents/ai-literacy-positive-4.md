# AI literacy measures

This note covers the staff and contractors who operate or use AI systems on our behalf.

Roles are grouped into three tiers according to how directly a person acts on the output of a model.

A written description of the AI literacy measures taken sits in section 4 of the staff handbook, and records of the guidance actually made available to staff are held by the people team.

The material is written in plain language and does not assume a technical background.
